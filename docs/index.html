How to Buy

Create a wallet
Download Phantom wallet app or wallet of choice from the App Store or Google Play Store for free. Desktop users, download the Google Chrome extension by going to phantom.com.
Buy some SOL's
Have SOL in your wallet to switch to $AFFAIR. If you don’t have any SOL, you can buy directly on Phantom wallet, transfer from another wallet, or buy on another exchange and send it to your wallet.
Buy in Coinbook
Connect to coinbook.fun. Search for AFFAIR token in the searchbar. Connect your wallet. Set how much SOL you want to use for buying AFFAIR. Place the trade to get them sweet AFFAIR coins.
